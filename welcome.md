# Applied Data Science

![](_static/quote.png)

Applied Data Science is about using data to understand problems, discover patterns, build models, and communicate useful insights.

This textbook provides a practical introduction to the methods and tools used throughout the data science workflow. Rather than treating statistical analysis, machine learning, and deep learning as separate subjects, we will examine how these approaches fit together as part of an iterative process of working with real-world data.

You will learn not only how to use data science methods, but also how to determine when they are appropriate, evaluate their results, recognize their limitations, and communicate what you have learned.

What You Will Learn

By working through this textbook, you will learn how to:

prepare, transform, and explore real-world data;
use visualization to identify patterns and communicate findings;
apply statistical methods to analyze relationships in data;
build and evaluate supervised and unsupervised machine learning models;
work with dimensionality reduction, clustering, recommendation, and sequential data;
develop an understanding of neural networks and deep learning;
recognize sources of bias, fairness concerns, and other ethical challenges in data science;
translate analytical results into meaningful and actionable insights.
